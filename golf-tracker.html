⛳ Golf Game Improvement Tracker

Log each hole, get an instant round summary and shot-pattern breakdown, and track your improvement round over round.

⚠️ Beta software — use at your own risk. This app is provided for informal, recreational use only, with no warranty as to the accuracy of scoring, handicap, or Stableford calculations.

Player Profile

Profiles are stored on this device only. To create a new profile, type your name next to the Profile dropdown above, fill in your details below, then tap "Save Profile". Next time, just pick your name from the dropdown (a simple "login"). Real OTP-verified accounts are a planned future upgrade.

Course Library (Admin)

Add a course that isn't in the suggested list, or set up its Par & Stroke Index scorecard once here so Round Entry can auto-fill it every time that course is played — no re-typing Stroke Index on the tee. Courses with several tees (Men's/Ladies, Club/Championship, etc.) auto-load the right one from the Gender and Tee Marker fields above.

Expected columns: club_name, course_layout, union, city, tee_name, tee_colour, player_category, hole, par, stroke_index, distance_metres, course_rating, slope_rating, scorecard_status. Re-importing a course replaces its previous data.

HoleParStroke Index

Saved courses show up in the Course suggestions below and their Par/Stroke Index load automatically once you pick that course, Gender, and Tee Marker in Round Entry / Player Profile.
